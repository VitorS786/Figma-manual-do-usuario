# TabMesa — Soft Collaboration Guidelines

## Palette
- Background: #F5F2EE (greige)
- Primary: #8B7FC7 (muted lavender)
- Secondary: #7E9478 (sage)
- Card: #FFFFFF
- Text: #3D3830 (warm charcoal)
- Muted: #9C9490
- Border: #E8E4DE
- Status — Livre: #7E9478 | Em Refeição: #8B7FC7 | Aguardando: #C8933A | Pago: #5BA85A

## Typography
- Face: Nunito (Google Fonts), weights 400 500 600 700 800
- Headings: Nunito 700–800, comfortable sizes
- Body: Nunito 400–500
- Labels/badges: Nunito 600, small caps feel

## Shape
- Card radius: 14px
- Button radius: 10px
- Input radius: 10px
- Badge radius: 8px

## Spacing
- Section padding: 24–32px
- Card padding: 20–24px
- Gap between cards: 16–20px

## Signature
- Rounded cards on warm greige ground
- Columns layout (kanban, grid)
- Lavender for primary actions, sage for success/confirmation
- Amber for waiting states, soft green for completed
